# Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

The **Coffee Shop Sales Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze coffee shop sales performance. The dashboard provides insights into total sales, transactions, quantity sold, peak sales hours, monthly sales trends, product size preferences, and product category performance.

The goal of this project is to transform sales data into meaningful insights that help understand customer purchasing behavior and overall business performance.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance and transaction volume.
- Identify peak sales hours throughout the day.
- Track monthly sales and transaction trends.
- Understand customer preferences based on product size.
- Compare sales across different product categories.
- Enable interactive analysis using product type and store location filters.

## Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays three key metrics:

- **Total Sales:** 698K
- **Total Transactions:** 149K
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 214K

### 2. Peak Sales Hours
Visualizes sales distribution across different times of the day:

- Morning
- Afternoon
- Evening

This helps identify the busiest periods and supports better staff scheduling and inventory planning.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trends
Displays monthly sales and transaction trends from January to June, helping track changes in business performance over time.

### 4. Sales by Size
A doughnut chart illustrates the distribution of sales across product sizes:

- Large
- Regular
- Not Defined
- Small

This helps understand product size preferences and identify opportunities to improve product offerings.

### 5. Sales by Product Category
Compares sales across categories such as:

- Coffee
- Tea
- Bakery
- Drinking Chocolate
- Coffee Beans

The visualization helps identify the categories contributing most to total sales.

### 6. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:

- **Product Type**
- **Store Location**

These filters allow users to explore sales performance across different products and store locations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** – Data transformation and preparation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, and doughnut charts

## Business Insights

- Morning records the highest sales among the three displayed time periods.
- Coffee is the leading product category by sales, followed by tea.
- Large and regular sizes together account for a significant share of the displayed size distribution.
- Monthly sales show an overall upward trend from January to June.
- The dashboard enables users to explore performance by product type and store location.

## How to Use

1. Download the Power BI report (`.pbix`) from this repository.
2. Open the file using Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Interact with the product type and store location slicers.
4. Explore the charts to analyze sales performance and identify trends.
